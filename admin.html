    Buttons, labels and messages. Changes show after Save texts. Tier names: the pencil next to a tier in Items.

    English is always the first language. ✓ = the page's own words (buttons, hints, errors) are already translated; you can tweak them in Texts → Small words.

    Fills in everything still missing in that language: title, lines, tiers, item names, descriptions, notes and shop labels. Nothing already written is touched. Review the result in Items and Texts.

    The currency follows the language you pick (rubles for Russian, hryvnias for Ukrainian, euros for German…). Picking a currency brings today's rate; ↻ fetches it again, and a rate you type wins. English prices are always USD. On the page, items without a typed second price show one converted from USD; in the editor, ≈ writes it in for you.
